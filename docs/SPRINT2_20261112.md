## 2. 3D Shelf case — EXPERIMENTAL, implemented, off on the stable branch
**Why it looked flat.** The pulled-out case was two separate flat pictures: the shelf's spine turned −90°·t round its own middle, and a front turned +90°→0 round its own middle, laid side by side (Batch 523's `shelfBoxFaces`: widths d·cos t and w·sin t). That gave:
- no perspective shared by the two faces;
- no top, bottom or back;
- no light change as a face turns.

**Now.** `ShelfCase3D.kt` with `ShelfPulled3D` in `ShelfHome.kt`. The pulled-out case is one rigid `Case3D`: the existing renderer with real front, spine, top, bottom, edge and back faces, real case thickness, light per face and custom spines kept.
- **Pose:** a pure function, `shelfCasePose`. It turns round its own upright from spine-on (yaw 90°) to front (0°), stays at one size while turning, then scales uniformly to its big place.
- **Tip:** up to 11° mid-turn so its top shows. The camera is level with the case's middle, so it needs more than 8.4°. It is level at both ends.
- **Perspective correction:** at the hand-over the 3D spine covers exactly the shelf's spine slot; at the end the front is exactly the big front.
- **Thickness:** taken from the slot (`Case3D(depthOverride)`).
- **No flash:** the 3D case takes over only once it reports itself whole (`onReady`) and still spine-on (`Shelf3D.decide`). If it isn't ready by the time the turn starts, that pull keeps the old path; it never swaps mid-turn.

**Switch:** `Shelf3D.enabled` is an internal flag, not a setting.
- `feature/popup-native`: **off**, so behaviour is unchanged.
- **`exp/shelf-3d-flip`** (10fff09, worktree `/home/claude/shelf3d`, local): **on**, as the review branch.
- QA app: `--ez voltcap_test_shelf3d true` turns it on.

**Evidence** (PC, not hardware):
- `ShelfCase3DTest`, 9 checks: hand-over = slot ±1 %; end = big front ±1 %; 1,000-step continuity; one rigid box (the spine and front share their edge to 0.01 px); never the back or the far edge; never a mirrored front; top only mid-turn; the way back retraces the way out; the hand-over rule.
- `docs/evidence/shelf3d_geometry_frames.png`: projected faces at shelf / extracting / partly out / turn 25-50-75 % / turned / out / back 75-25 % / returned.
- `ShelfCase3DRenderTest`: **the real `Case3D` drawn by Android's graphics** (Robolectric native graphics) at 8 poses → `docs/evidence/shelf3d_render_android.png`. The drawn width grows from spine to two faces to the front, as asserted. No cover art is loaded in the test, so the spine is the dark stand-in.

**Not verified (hardware pending):** the look with real art on the KONKR / Flip 2 GPU, frame pacing, the ~1-frame difference between the shelf spine's drawn top cap and the 3D case's level top at the hand-over, and how it feels. ## 4. Spine flicker — PC findings (not claimed fixed on the device)
`SpineCacheLifecycleRoboTest` runs the real `rememberDerived` (Case3D's spine pipeline) and records the spine on every frame. It passes 5/5:
- the same art decoded again → never EMPTY;
- custom spine A → B → never A's spine for B;
- back to a game already made → its spine at once, before its picture decodes;
- rapid A B C A D B → settles on B;
- a new spine → the dark strip for at most 3 frames.

So the cache lifecycle has no texture gap on the PC. If the owner's flicker persists on the device, `SpineTrace` (EMPTY / SWAP / REDECODE, QA trace only, no cost in a normal build) will show whether it's the texture. If none of those is logged during the flicker, it's lighting or compositing.
