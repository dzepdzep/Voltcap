## 2. Real-window behaviour tests (Phases 2, 3)
`WinFocusRoboTest` composes the **real `GameWindow`** of every layout on the JVM (640 × 427 dp). Keys arrive through the window's own dialog, as a controller's do, and frames are stepped one at a time with focus checked on every frame. 53 layouts, plus 3 with layout windows off (the plain window), × 6 tests:
- opens on Play, and Play works after the open guard;
- B from Options lands on the opener on **every** frame, never on Play;
- rapid B then A never starts the game;
- B in the middle of a page change;
- an A 100 ms after B acts on the opener (the one-press rule);
- L1/R1 walk the pages (native-page layouts).

**It found three real bugs, all fixed:**
1. **TV room: an A 100 ms after B started the game.** B from Game options came back onto Play. The plain window had the same gap. Details, Game options and Achievements now register as the page's opener (`PageOpeners`, `WinPageFlow.kt`).
2. **Native pages lost L1/R1 presses during a page change.** A leftover 60 ms focus wait left nothing focused for about 4 frames. It's removed for native pages; the shared page keeps it.
3. **Night menu at ×1.3: "ACHIEVEMENTS" was cut**, wrapping to a second line its 26 dp key couldn't show. It's now one line that shrinks readably, then scrolls (rule 11). Found by `ShelfTextRoboTest`.

`WinPresentationTest`: what A opens is now one decision, `winPresentation()`, read by `GameWindow` (Classic → Glance → row motion → banner → own window/plain). It is tested for every layout × every combination of the 5 popup-style prefs (11,872 cases), plus layout identity (the 4 native scenes keep every page; the others get the shared page for Details, Achievements and Options; Cross keeps its list).

## 5. Spine flicker (Phase 6) — NOT claimed fixed
With the QA trace on, each game's spine texture changes are logged as **EMPTY** (back to the dark strip), **SWAP** (another source), **REDECODE** (a new copy) or **FILLED** (`SpineTrace`, unit-tested). A flicker in a capture with none of these logged points at lighting or compositing, not the texture. That needs a device capture.
