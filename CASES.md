# Voltcap: real game cases (update 191)

What Voltcap's 3D cases are drawn from (`src\ui\CaseCatalog.kt`). Four research passes looked up each console's
packaging per region (Wikipedia's keep case, video game packaging and optical disc packaging articles, protector and
replacement-case makers' sizes, MobyGames, Nintendo Life, The Dreamcast Junkyard, Digital Game Museum, collector
forums). Only how the cases are built was studied; no artwork was copied.

**How sure:**
- **Measured:** someone measured a real one.
- **Published size:** a standard size (DVD / Blu-ray keep case, CD jewel case) or a maker's listed size.
- **Estimated:** worked out, usually from the inside of a protector made for that box (the box is 1–2 mm smaller).
- **Placeholder:** nothing was found. The nearest known case is drawn, and it is not finished work.

Colours are matched from photos and descriptions, not measured. Hinges, catches and clip positions are mostly
unconfirmed; where a detail wasn't found it isn't drawn, or the note says it's assumed.

Sizes are width × height × thickness in mm, as the case stands.

**No retail case (none invented):** Steam, Android / apps, Arcade, Naomi, Atomiswave, PICO-8, ScummVM. They keep
Voltcap's own case, which is Voltcap's design and not presented as a real one.

**Not modelled (no data found):** MSX cartridge boxes in Japan (the cassette case is drawn there too, a placeholder),
Famicom Disk System and 64DD packaging. Not drawn yet:
- Platinum / Greatest Hits / Players' Choice colours;
- Japanese obi strips and outer sleeves;
- early PS1 and TurboGrafx-CD longboxes;
- late cardboard Mega Drive boxes;
- the later, smaller Lynx boxes.

## What's drawn from it
- **Shape and thickness:** the real case's, for the game's region.
- **Plastic:** its colour and how see-through it is. The edges of a see-through case are deeper in colour.
- **Thumb grip:** on keep and card cases only.
- **Jewel cases:** the 120 mm booklet under the clear lid, and the tray's hinge ridge in the tray's colour.
- **Cardboard:** matte, printed edges. Opens with the tuck flap and dust flaps on top; the inner tray rises.
- **Inside:**
  - the disc at its real size on its hub (8 cm on GameCube);
  - the card at its real size and place (Switch low right, DS top with the GBA slot under it);
  - the disc on the left for Xbox One / Series;
  - memory card holders on PS2 and GameCube (their place is assumed).

## Verification checklist
1. The type-check passes (0 errors). Done for update 191.
2. Every console key Voltcap knows is in the table, has no retail case, or is listed as not modelled. Done.
3. Regions read from file names: (USA), (Europe), (Japan), (World) → the default; Settings › Game look › Covers,
   cases & discs › Case region.
4. On a device: a PAL vs US PS1 case (thickness), and a US vs EU DS case (black vs clear).
5. On a device: an Xbox One / Series case opens with the disc on the left.
6. On a device: a GameCube case shows an 8 cm disc. A Switch case shows the card low on the right.
7. On a device: a NES, SNES or Game Boy box opens by its top flap, the tray rises and the cartridge comes out.
8. On a device: a jewel case shows the whole booklet with the ridge beside it, nothing cut.
9. On a device: an Android game with a custom cartridge shows a cartridge in its case (not a disc).
10. Placeholders stay marked here until real measurements replace them.

## Opening edges (update 193)
- **Hinged cases:** the right side shows the front lid's edge, a fine seam and the rear shell's lip. The seam's place
  is worked out from the lid's thickness, as follows:
  - keep cases: about 2 mm;
  - jewel lids: 2.4 mm;
  - DS / 3DS / Vita halves: about even;
  - clamshells: even.
- **Catches:** two on keep and card cases, the two lid tabs on jewel cases, one on clamshells. Their positions are
  approximate, not measured.
- **Cardboard boxes:** no side opening. The top shows its back fold and the tuck flap's slit, and it opens there.
- **Opening:** the lid has its own thickness and swings on the hinge; the tray's walls stop where it sat.
- **Not yet:** rounded corners, the PAL Dreamcast multi-part case, and NA vs EU DS closure differences beyond
  thickness.
