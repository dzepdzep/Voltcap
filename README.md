# Voltcap

**A game launcher for Android handhelds.** Voltcap turns your ROM folders, Android games and Steam games into one
library that looks like a shelf of real games: covers, cases, cartridges and discs you can pick up, turn round and open.
It's made for controllers first (touch works everywhere) and for handhelds like the AYN Thor, the Odin 2 Portal and the
Retroid Pocket, including dual-screen ones.

## Getting started

1. Download the latest APK from [Releases](https://github.com/dzepdzep/Voltcap/releases) and install it.
2. Open Voltcap and follow the first-time setup: point it at your ROM folders, pick your emulators and choose a look.
3. Press Home and choose Voltcap as your launcher if you like (you can switch back any time).

Voltcap tells you when a new version is out and can update itself (Settings › Updates & downloads).

## Features

### Your library
- ROM folders (several, deep scan, multi-disc games merged), installed Android games, Steam games and imports from
  other frontends — in one list.
- Categories by console, collections and smart categories (Continue playing, Rediscover…), play status, sorting and
  search.
- Play time and last played for every game, Android games included, synced between your devices.
- Art and details from SteamGridDB, ScreenScraper, IGDB and more, including animated covers, game manuals and clips.

### Real games on your screen
- Every game as its cover, its real case, or its cartridge, card or disc — each console's own shapes and sizes.
- Open a game and its piece comes out to be looked at: turn it round, open the case, take the disc out, read the
  manual inside.
- Drawn cartridges for dozens of consoles with researched fronts and backs (screws, labels, connectors), or your own
  cartridge pack, clear plastic or any colour — any game can go on any shell.

### Home layouts
- Over 25 layouts: a classic row, carousels, grids, a TV room, a dashboard, side and cross menus, case stacks, a
  bookshelf, a record crate, a disc station, a circuit board, a 2001-style green machine and a 32-bit handheld-RPG menu,
  and more — each with its own options.
- Four simple looks for the games (Covers, Cases, Cartridges & discs, Art) with live previews.
- More than 100 animated themes, your own wallpaper (picture, GIF or video), interface styles, sounds and music.

### Achievements and saves
- RetroAchievements and Steam achievements: progress, missables, unlock animations, badge walls and recaps.
- Save Vault: every save and memory card in one place, synced to Google Drive (saves only, never save states).

### Second screen
- On dual-screen handhelds: art, video, details with the case to open, achievements, a music page with synced lyrics
  and visualizers, your games as folders, and a dock with your own shortcuts.

### Your handheld
- Performance tiers, AutoTDP, underclocking per CPU cluster and GPU, fan curves and per-game profiles.
- Controller lights, a real charge limit, a volume mixer, an in-game overlay and Quick Settings tiles.

## Privacy

Voltcap keeps your data on your device; accounts you connect (Google Drive, RetroAchievements, art sources) are only
used for what you turn on. See [PRIVACY.md](PRIVACY.md).
