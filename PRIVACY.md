# Voltcap — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

Voltcap is a game launcher for Android handhelds. It is made by one person and has no servers of its own.

## What Voltcap collects

Nothing is sent to the developer. Voltcap has no analytics, no ads and no tracking. Your library, settings, play times and art stay on your device.

## Google Drive

If you choose to use Google Drive (Settings › Backup › Export to Google Drive, the first-start restore, or save sync), Voltcap asks for the `drive.file` permission. With it, Voltcap can only see and change the files it created itself, in the folder **Voltcap** in your own Google Drive. It cannot see any of your other files.

- **What it stores there:** your Voltcap backup (`voltcap-backup.json`) and, if you turn on save sync, your game saves.
- **Who can read it:** only you, through your Google account. The developer never receives it.
- **Removing access:** delete the Voltcap folder in your Drive, and remove Voltcap's access at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Voltcap's use of information received from Google APIs follows the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.

## Other services

Voltcap talks to other services only when you use a feature that needs them, and directly from your device. These include RetroAchievements, ScreenScraper, SteamGridDB, IGDB, TheGamesDB, Steam, Spotify and RomM. The keys and sign-ins you enter for them are stored only on your device, and each service's own privacy policy applies to what you send it.

## Children

Voltcap is not aimed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect anything from them.
