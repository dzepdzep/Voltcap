# Voltcap: cartridge collection (update 195)

Every cartridge / card shell Voltcap draws itself (`src\ui\CartShells.kt`), and how sure each one is. Three research
passes used ConsoleMods' dimension table, NESdev, SMS Power caliper measurements, Wikipedia, AtariAge, museum
catalogues and label-size threads. Photos (Evan-Amos on Wikimedia Commons) couldn't be measured from here, so outlines
are drawn from the measured sizes plus how the shells look, and are marked "partly estimated".

**Status:**
- **Drawn:** size from a measurement or published standard.
- **Drawn, partly estimated:** the outer size is measured; the outline details, label place, ridges and screws are
  estimated.
- **Placeholder, not finished:** nothing was measured. The size and shape are a best guess and still need real
  references.

Sizes are width × height × thickness in mm, front view.

## How it works
- **Any game on any shell:** use the game's menu › Cartridge shell…, then pick one under Authentic (by maker,
  searchable) or Your custom shells (your cartridge pack's 14 shapes).
  - **For one game or all of a console's games:** "For every <console> game" switches between the two.
  - **Colour:** the shell's own, its real variants (gold, translucent…), or any of black, grey, white, gold, clear,
    smoke, and clear red / blue / green / purple / yellow. A see-through shell shows its board inside.
  - **What changes:** only how the game is drawn. Its console, emulator, file and launch never change.
- **The label:**
  - it sits only inside the shell's label area, never over the plastic;
  - it shows the game's cover, cut to the label's shape (never stretched), under a band with the game's real
    console's name (a PS2 game on an N64 shell says PlayStation 2);
  - with no cover, it shows the game's name.
- **3D:** turned in the game window, a shell is solid at its real thickness. Its back shows the seam, the screws and
  the contacts.
- **Authentic drawn cartridges** (Settings › Game look › Covers, cases & discs): every cartridge console's games go
  on its own real shell, by region (NES / Famicom, US SNES / Super Famicom-PAL, Western / Japanese Mega Drive,
  Western / Japanese Master System). Off by default, so your template cartridges stay as they were.
- **Scanned cartridges:** a game's own scanned cartridge picture is still the "Real discs" option, separate from the
  shells.

## Not done yet (Parts 1 and 2 aren't finished until these are)
- **Placeholders:** every shell marked Placeholder above needs real measurements.
- **Missing shells:** Famicom publisher-specific shells, the Atari 2600 Frogger-style long shell, NEC Bee Card,
  WonderSwan Color / SwanCrystal differences, Neo Geo MVS revisions, and Casio PV-1000 vs Loopy details.
- **Special parts not modelled in 3D:**
  - the Game Boy power-lock notch;
  - the Sonic & Knuckles flap opening;
  - the J-Cart's ports, which are drawn on the front, not the top;
  - the Game Boy Camera ball's depth.
- **3D models from your shape library:** in the picker since update 197 (Your 3D pieces). They're drawn from their
  pre-rendered angles, as the 3D models look draws them.
- **Device check:** none of this has been tested on a device yet.

## Verification checklist (per shell)
1. Width, height and thickness: measured where marked.
2. Front outline: estimated, to be checked against photos.
3. Side profile: a straight extrusion. Tapers and bevels are not modelled yet.
4. Back, screws and seam: drawn where known, estimated.
5. Connector: only the contacts seen on cards and HuCards. The opening at the bottom isn't modelled.
6. Label area and mask: from measured sticker sizes where marked (NES, Famicom, Game Boy, Genesis, 32X, Game Gear,
   Virtual Boy), estimated otherwise.
7. Colours: matched by eye.
8. Regional variants: NES / Famicom, SNES NA / PAL-SFC, Mega Drive West / Japan, Master System West / Japan,
   Game Boy Color black / clear.
9. Custom shells: your pack's shapes are in the same picker.
10. Cross-console labels: the game's real console is shown; no game data changes.
